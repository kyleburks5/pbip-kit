# Known gaps of the pbir CLI (history)

pbip-kit used the pbir CLI (0.9.32) up to v0.2. From v0.3 the kit builds with its own tool, pbik (tools/pbik), and
never runs pbir (CLAUDE.md). These rows were in `design/known-gaps.md`; they describe pbir's behaviour, not Desktop's,
and are kept as history for the Rep 1-5 scripts in `archive/examples/` (home copy of the kit, not shipped). Helper names `pbir_helpers.<name>` refer to the v0.2
helpers; their pure-Python parts are now in `tools/pbik/pbikit/objects.py` (`design/scripts/pbik_helpers.py`).

Now handled by pbik (tests in tools/pbik/tests): numbers in filters and slicer selections are typed from the TMDL
(`2024L`); page navigation targets the page name; per-visual field renames (`displayName` on the binding); slicer
default selections; overlapping visuals; idempotent reference lines and image registration; data-bar `hideText`;
measure-driven titles, subtitles and axis values; extension measures with a data type. What pbik can't write yet is
in `design/known-gaps.md`.
